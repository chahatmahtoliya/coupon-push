Amazon Welcome Offers: Coupons for New Users in India

Amazon India savings are usually attached to products, memberships, payment methods or limited campaigns rather than one permanent first-shopping coupon.

CouponPush Editorial · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

Last verified: 16 Sep 2026 (IST). Terms change per campaign — confirm the discount line at checkout.

Searching for a Amazon welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerAmazon India does not publish one universal welcome coupon for every first shopping order. Look for Collect Coupon boxes on product pages, new-member Prime promotions, Amazon Pay offers and eligible bank discounts.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • Collect the coupon before adding or checking out when required.
  • Buy the exact eligible product from the participating seller.
  • Meet the offer's minimum purchase or membership conditions.
  • Use the named payment method within the campaign period.

How to apply the Amazon welcome offer

  1. Sign in and open the exact product page.
  2. Look for a coupon checkbox or promotion beneath the price.
  3. Read the seller, quantity and expiry conditions.
  4. Collect the coupon and review the order summary.
  5. Add a bank or Amazon Pay offer only when checkout confirms it.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

Product coupons are not account-wide welcome coupons

An Amazon coupon can apply to one product or seller while another version of the same item remains ineligible. New Prime-member promotions are also separate from first shopping orders. The article and store page should never combine these into a guaranteed new-user saving.

Why the Amazon welcome coupon may not work

  • The coupon was not collected.
  • A different seller or product variation is selected.
  • The promotion requires more than one unit.
  • The account is not eligible for a targeted campaign.
  • The order was placed after the offer period or allocation ended.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Does Amazon India give every new user a first-order coupon?

No permanent universal first-order shopping coupon is publicly guaranteed.

How do Amazon product coupons work?

Collect the coupon on an eligible product page and confirm that it appears in the order summary.

Is a Prime welcome offer the same thing?

No. A Prime membership promotion concerns membership eligibility and should not be described as a general shopping coupon.

Amazon welcome coupon FAQs

Does Amazon have a first-order coupon code in India?

Amazon runs new-user offers for eligible first orders in India, but the live code, minimum order value and discount cap change by campaign. Open the current offer terms on the Amazon app or website and confirm the discount line in your bag before paying.

What minimum order applies to the Amazon welcome coupon?

The minimum eligible basket for a Amazon welcome coupon is set per campaign and usually counts only coupon-eligible items. If your bag holds excluded styles, restaurants or products, the eligible subtotal can fall below the threshold even when the total looks high enough.

Why is my Amazon coupon code not working?

The usual reasons are: the account or device is not treated as new, the bag contains excluded items, the eligible subtotal is below the minimum, the code was entered on the wrong platform (app vs website), or the campaign ended. Remove excluded items and re-apply the code shown to your signed-in account.

Does the Amazon welcome offer work on the app and the website?

Not always. Some Amazon new-user codes are app-only while others are website-only, and the eligible catalogue can differ between platforms. Check the offer terms for the channel restriction and apply the code on the correct platform.

Can I stack a bank offer with the Amazon welcome coupon?

Sometimes. Apply the welcome coupon first, then proceed to payment and check whether an eligible bank or wallet offer appears on the final order summary. Never count both savings until the checkout displays them together.
