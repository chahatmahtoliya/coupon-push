Smartphone deal FAQs

Will phone prices drop in the sales?

No model-wise 2026 price is confirmed in this guide. Compare the live product price with your baseline.

Should I buy on the first day?

Buy early only when the exact item and total meet your target. Speed is not a substitute for checking the seller, variant and terms.

Do exchange offers stack with sale prices?

Only when the live merchant terms allow it. The final value can change after inspection, so compare it with selling the old phone separately.

How do payment offers change the deal?

Use only the current offer shown for your card or EMI method. Read the bank guide.

Where can I track live phone prices?

Use CouponPush offers and the Flipkart and Amazon pages, then verify the final summary.
