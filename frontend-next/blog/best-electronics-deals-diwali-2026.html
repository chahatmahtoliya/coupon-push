EMI and warranty checks

  1. Compare the EMI total with full payment; read processing fees and GST.
  2. Check whether selecting EMI removes a coupon or changes the payment offer.
  3. Price extended warranty and installation separately.
  4. Confirm delivery and exchange terms for your pin code.
  5. Run the final figure through the savings calculator.
Source status: Amazon has published its GIF start and SBI card/EMI offer. Flipkart lists October 8 early access, October 9 public access and up to 10% on eligible Axis and ICICI cards. Check both live payment pages for caps and exclusions.

Electronics deal FAQs

Is the Diwali period the best time to buy electronics?

It can be competitive, but no calendar alone proves a deal. Compare your baseline with the exact all-in total.

Do exchange offers apply to laptops and TVs?

Only where the listing and inspection terms allow it. Compare the supported value with selling separately.

Is no-cost EMI actually free?

Check the loan summary, fees, GST and any discount removed when EMI is selected.

Should I buy extended warranty?

Compare its price and coverage with the manufacturer's warranty and the seller's service terms.

Where can I track live electronics prices?

Use CouponPush offers and the Flipkart and Amazon pages.
