Big Billion Days vs Great Indian Festival 2026: Where to Buy

There is no single winner — category patterns plus a 5-minute all-in comparison method for any product.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · 8 min read

Last verified: 16 Sep 2026 (IST). BBD facts confirmed; GIF dates and bank partner awaited — the method below works regardless.

On this page Quick answer The all-in method Category patterns Timing strategy FAQs

Flipkart Big Billion Days (confirmed: Oct 9, early access Oct 8, 10% Axis/ICICI) and Amazon Great Indian Festival (dates awaited, expected late Sept) overlap every year. Headline prices often match — the winner is decided by coupons, bank partners and exchange terms on your exact variant.

Quick answer Compare all-in totals per variant, not headlines. BBD's confirmed edge: 10% Axis/ICICI instant discount. GIF's typical edge: broader electronics catalogue plus Prime early access. Split your list across both sales and buy limited-stock flagships in the earliest window you can access.

The all-in comparison method (5 minutes)

  1. Fix the exact variant (storage, colour, seller) on both platforms.
  2. Note each sale price, then apply coupons — see the Flipkart guide and Amazon guide for mechanics.
  3. Subtract the bank offer using its minimum and cap — see the bank offers guide.
  4. Add delivery, exchange deductions and warranty differences.
  5. Run both totals through the savings calculator and buy the lower one.
