Swiggy Welcome Coupon: First-Order Terms Explained

Swiggy welcome offers can differ by account, city, restaurant and service. Always use the offer shown in the signed-in app for the intended order.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 3 July 2026 · 7 min read
On this page Quick answer Offer types Eligibility How to apply Why it may fail FAQs

Searching for a Swiggy welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerThere is no single permanent Swiggy welcome code that can be guaranteed for every new account. Check the app's Offers section after selecting the delivery address and restaurant. Referral credit and coupon discounts may not be combinable.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The delivery address must be inside the promotion area.
  • The selected restaurant must participate.
  • The eligible food subtotal must meet the threshold.
  • The account and payment method must satisfy the offer terms.

How to apply the Swiggy welcome offer

  1. Set the correct delivery address first.
  2. Choose a restaurant and add the intended items.
  3. Open View all coupons in the cart.
  4. Compare the new-user, restaurant and payment options.
  5. Apply one offer and verify fees plus the final payable total.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

Swiggy eligibility changes after restaurant selection

A coupon visible on the home screen can disappear when the chosen restaurant, delivery distance or cart value does not qualify. Packaging, platform and delivery fees may also sit outside the coupon calculation.

Why the Swiggy welcome coupon may not work

  • The restaurant does not accept the offer.
  • The food subtotal excludes fees and is below the threshold.
  • The delivery location is outside the campaign.
  • Referral credit is already applied.
  • The offer is restricted to online payment or a named method.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find a Swiggy welcome offer?

Set the address, build the cart and open the available coupons in the signed-in app.

Can referral credit and a coupon be combined?

Swiggy's published coupon policy says initial referral value may not be used with another coupon discount.

Do delivery fees count toward minimum order?

Usually the eligible food subtotal is the relevant figure; read the specific coupon terms.
