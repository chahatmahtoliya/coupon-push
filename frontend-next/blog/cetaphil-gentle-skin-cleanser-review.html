Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser Review: Who Is It For?

A close reading of the India formula, its best fit and its trade-offs before you buy.

CouponPush Editorial·24 Sep 2026·5 min read

Source-based review of Cetaphil India's published product details; we have not used or checkout-tested this product.

On this pageQuick verdictFormulaWho it suitsTrade-offsBuying checksQuestions

Cetaphil's Gentle Skin Cleanser is positioned in India as a creamy daily cleanser for dry to normal, sensitive skin. This review evaluates the published formula and product claims, not a personal wear test. The official Cetaphil India product page is the source for the features and ingredient list below.

Quick verdictIt is a sensible shortlist choice if you want a creamy, fragrance-free cleanser and prefer a gentle cleanse over a strong foaming feel. If excess oil is your main concern, compare Cetaphil's oily-skin gel before buying.

What is in the India formula?

The current India product page lists glycerin, niacinamide and panthenol among its ingredients. Cetaphil describes the cleanser as creamy and says it removes dirt, makeup and impurities while helping skin feel hydrated. The same page labels it fragrance free and paraben free. Ingredient lists can change, so compare the pack you receive with the online listing.

The absence of a perfume ingredient is useful information if you actively avoid fragrance. It does not guarantee that every individual will tolerate the formula. If you know you react to a particular ingredient, read the full list on the pack before use.

Who is it likely to suit?

  • Dry to normal skin: this is the skin-type range Cetaphil names for this product.
  • People who prefer little lather: a creamy cleanser is a different experience from a foaming gel.
  • Fragrance avoiders: the India listing specifically calls this formula fragrance free.

Those are shopping fit signals, not a diagnosis or a promise that the product will solve irritation. Skin response varies, especially when the rest of a routine includes strong active ingredients.

Where the review has limits

We did not measure cleansing performance, test it on makeup, or compare skin feel after weeks of use. Cetaphil's efficacy statements are brand claims on its own page. The information here helps you decide whether the formula is worth considering, but it cannot substitute for your own patch test or a clinician's advice about a persistent skin problem.

If you want a cleanser specifically aimed at removing excess oil, the brand positions its Oily Skin Cleanser differently. Our Gentle vs Oily Skin Cleanser comparison lays out the differences in skin-type guidance, texture and fragrance ingredients.

How to compare value before buying

  1. Choose the same size and formula at each retailer; a lower price on a smaller pack may cost more per 100 ml.
  2. Check the seller, delivery charge, return rules and final payable amount.
  3. If a retailer advertises a Cetaphil discount code, apply it in that retailer's checkout and confirm the total changes.

Our Cetaphil coupon code and offers page is the place to check current listings. At the time of this review it did not confirm a public code, so we have not invented one here. Cetaphil's India site also offers a Where to Buy route.

Common questions

Is Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser fragrance free in India?

Yes, the current official India listing labels this specific Gentle Skin Cleanser formula fragrance free. Check the exact pack and variant when ordering.

Is this a review from hands-on testing?

No. It is an editorial assessment of the official India listing and ingredient information. We have not used the cleanser ourselves.

Is it the same as Oily Skin Cleanser?

No. Cetaphil positions the Gentle version for dry to normal sensitive skin and the Oily version for combination to oily sensitive skin. Their textures and ingredient lists also differ.
