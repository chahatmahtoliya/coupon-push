FirstCry Welcome Coupon: New-User Eligibility and Savings

FirstCry coupon value depends heavily on category rules. Baby food, feeding products and selected merchandise may be excluded even when the overall cart is large enough.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 3 July 2026 · 7 min read
On this page Quick answer Offer types Eligibility How to apply Why it may fail FAQs

Searching for a FirstCry welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerCheck the current FirstCry Offer Zone or account coupons for a new-user promotion. FirstCry's published coupon policy says offers can be limited to selected products, used once per user or household, and generally cannot be combined with another coupon.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The coupon must be available to the account or household.
  • Products, sizes and colours must be listed as eligible.
  • The net eligible amount must meet the threshold.
  • Only one coupon may be permitted on the order.

How to apply the FirstCry welcome offer

  1. Open the Offer Zone or My Account coupon area.
  2. Read the excluded category list before building the cart.
  3. Check coupon eligibility for each size and colour.
  4. Apply the code and review the net merchandise value.
  5. Recheck shipping and cash-on-delivery charges after the discount.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

FirstCry exclusions matter more than the headline cart value

FirstCry's terms identify exclusions that can include baby food, infant formula, feeding bottles, innerwear and selected products. If these items dominate the cart, the eligible value may sit below the coupon threshold.

Why the FirstCry welcome coupon may not work

  • The user or household has already used the offer.
  • The cart contains excluded baby-care categories.
  • The selected size or colour is outside the offer.
  • Another coupon is already applied.
  • The eligible net value is below the minimum.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Can a FirstCry welcome coupon cover baby food?

Do not assume so. FirstCry's general coupon terms identify baby food and infant formula among categories that can be excluded.

Can two FirstCry coupons be combined?

The general policy says coupon offers cannot be clubbed unless a specific offer states otherwise.

Do shipping rules use the pre-discount total?

FirstCry says shipping and cash-on-delivery rules can apply to the net amount after the coupon.
