Shopping insights

Look beyond the headline discount.

A useful coupon comparison starts with your basket and ends with the amount you pay.

Editorial updateThis page previously contained coupon-market statistics. The source data is not available in this project, so those figures have been removed.

Compare the same basket

Use the same products and quantities when comparing offers. A larger discount can still leave you paying more if the original price is higher.

Check the cap

A percentage coupon may stop saving you money once it reaches its maximum discount. Read both the percentage and the limit.

Include the extra costs

Add delivery and other checkout fees. Check whether excluded items count toward any minimum spend.

Keep cashback separate

An immediate discount reduces the amount paid today. Cashback may arrive later or carry restrictions. Check the terms before treating them as equivalent.
