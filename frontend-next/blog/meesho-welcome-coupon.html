Meesho Welcome Coupon: First-Purchase Discount Guide

Meesho promotes first-order savings, but the available benefit can vary by account, product and campaign. Verify the live app banner and checkout total.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 3 July 2026 · 7 min read
On this page Quick answer Offer types Eligibility How to apply Why it may fail FAQs

Searching for a Meesho welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerMeesho has advertised first-order savings, including 'up to' percentage offers, but no single permanent public code should be assumed. Check the new account's app banner, product page and cart.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The account must be identified as eligible for the campaign.
  • The catalogue and product variation must participate.
  • The cart must meet any minimum merchandise value.
  • The offer must still be active for the delivery location.

How to apply the Meesho welcome offer

  1. Register and verify the new Meesho account.
  2. Open the welcome banner or Coupons section.
  3. Add only products displaying the applicable promotion.
  4. Review delivery charges separately from the merchandise saving.
  5. Confirm the applied discount before placing the order.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

An 'up to' offer is not a flat cart discount

The highest advertised percentage may be restricted by product, value or maximum saving. Delivery charges also affect the payable total. Compare the post-discount delivered price rather than treating the banner percentage as guaranteed.

Why the Meesho welcome coupon may not work

  • The product catalogue does not participate.
  • The offer is targeted to selected new accounts.
  • A minimum order condition has not been met.
  • The promotion is unavailable for the selected location.
  • The campaign ended or changed before checkout.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one Meesho welcome code?

A single permanent public code could not be verified. Use the offer presented inside the signed-in app.

Can delivery charges reduce the benefit?

Yes. Compare the complete payable total, including delivery and handling charges.

Will every product receive the advertised percentage?

No. 'Up to' promotions can vary by catalogue and maximum discount.
