Hostinger renewal price — the real cost

Hostinger’s model: deep first-term discount → standard renewal. This is not hidden, but it is easy to miss. Check hPanel → Billing → Subscriptions → Renewal price after purchase and set a calendar reminder 30 days before renewal.

  • Renewal coupons do not lower your existing plan’s renewal — codes apply to new purchases only.
  • Cheapest long-term move: longest first term you can afford (48-mo Premium/Business) + live referral stack + migrate/upgrade before renewal instead of auto-renewing blindly.
  • VPS renews at a smaller multiple (~1.7×) than shared (~3–4×) but needs server skills.

Hostinger review India (honest, 2026)

Rating snapshot: ~4.6–4.7/5 on Trustpilot-style aggregators; hPanel and speed praised, renewal jump and no phone support criticised. Our take after comparing specs and independent tests:

  • Speed: LiteSpeed + NVMe + Cloudflare CDN. Typical TTFB ~130–350ms in tests — faster than GoDaddy baseline, slightly ahead of Bluehost shared.
  • Uptime: ~99.9%+ claimed; independent trackers commonly show 99.95–99.98% on shared.
  • Ease of use: hPanel is cleaner than cPanel for beginners. 1-click WordPress, AI builder, free SSL auto-installed.
  • Support: 24/7 live chat (usually <2 min). No phone support — dealbreaker if you need voice help in regional languages.
  • Backups/security: Weekly on Single/Premium, daily + on-demand on Business/Cloud. Malware scanner basic; advanced security paid.

Pros: lowest entry price, generous storage, free SSL + CDN + email, Mumbai-friendly routing, beginner UI.
Cons: steep renewal (esp. Single), upsells at checkout, phone support absent, cheapest tier resource-capped, domain free only year 1.

Verdict: best value for bloggers, affiliates, students and small shops in India who are comfortable with chat support and a 48-month lock-in. Skip if you need phone hand-holding (pick Bluehost) or already live heavily in GoDaddy domains.

Why your Hostinger coupon code may not work

  • You typed an expired affiliate code from an old blog — the campaign ended.
  • You are logged into an existing account trying to apply a new-customer referral to a renewal — renewals are excluded.
  • You selected 1-month/monthly billing where the campaign requires 12+ months.
  • You opened Hostinger.com (global USD) instead of Hostinger India (INR) — prices and codes are region-locked.
  • Ad-blocker or cached page hid the code field — try incognito with the referral link, not manual typing.

Fix: clear excluded add-ons, switch to 48-month term, use the live link from the Hostinger store page, and confirm the order summary shows the reduced total before UPI/card OTP.

Hostinger India FAQs — Q&A

What is the best Hostinger coupon code for India in 2026?

No permanent code. The 64–83% discount auto-applies. Extra 10–20% referral codes work only when a campaign is live — check CouponPush’s Hostinger page first.

Do I need a promo code to get up to 75% off?

No. Select the term and the strikethrough price drops automatically. The code field is optional and seasonal.

What is Hostinger Premium vs Business (Unlimited) — which should I buy?

Premium for blogs/portfolios (weekly backups, 100 GB). Business/Unlimited for stores/client work (daily backups, more resources, better WooCommerce handling).

What is the Hostinger renewal price after 48 months?

Single ~₹289/mo, Premium ~₹449/mo, Business ~₹649/mo, Cloud Startup ~₹1,599/mo, plus 18% GST. Year-5 Single costs more than its first 4 years combined.

Does Hostinger India price include GST?

No. Displayed monthly rates exclude GST. Add 18% to upfront totals (e.g. ₹7,152 becomes ~₹8,439).

Is the free domain really free?

First year only, on Premium+ annual plans. Year 2 onward you pay the standard domain renewal.

Hostinger vs Bluehost — which is better for WordPress beginners?

Hostinger for budget + speed; Bluehost for guided WordPress onboarding + phone support + WordPress.org recommendation.

Hostinger vs GoDaddy — which is cheaper long-term?

Hostinger, in both intro and renewal, and it bundles SSL/email/CDN that GoDaddy often charges for.

Is there a Hostinger student discount or free hosting?

No permanent student/free tier. Cheapest student path: 48-mo Premium during Black Friday/Diwali + referral stack + 30-day guarantee as safety net.

When is Hostinger cheapest — Black Friday or Diwali?

Black Friday is usually lowest globally; Diwali/Monsoon is lowest India-specific window. Both beat buying off-season on 12 months.

Can I use a Hostinger coupon for renewal?

No. Codes apply to new purchases/upgrades, not existing-plan renewals. To save at renewal, extend/migrate before expiry or upgrade tier during a sale.

Does Hostinger offer monthly billing?

Short terms exist but at much higher per-month rates and often without the free domain. The advertised ₹69–₹149 rates require 48 months.

Which payment methods work in India — UPI, RuPay?

Yes: UPI, RuPay, Visa/Mastercard, netbanking options, PayPal and Google Pay on most checkouts.

Can I get a refund if I picked the wrong plan?

Eligible hosting has a 30-day money-back window. Upgrade/downgrade inside hPanel instead of cancelling if you just need more resources.
