Zomato Welcome Coupon: New-User Rules and Restrictions

Zomato promotions are dynamic. The new-user offer shown to one account or delivery location may not appear with another restaurant or at another time.

CouponPush Editorial · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

Last verified: 16 Sep 2026 (IST). Terms change per campaign — confirm the discount line at checkout.

On this page Quick answer Offer types Eligibility How to apply Why it may fail FAQs

Searching for a Zomato welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerUse the welcome offer displayed inside the signed-in Zomato account rather than relying on a fixed third-party code. Restaurant participation, address, eligible subtotal, time window and payment method can all change the result.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The account must receive the promotion.
  • The selected restaurant or outlet must participate.
  • The eligible food value must meet the minimum.
  • The order must fall inside any time and location restrictions.

How to apply the Zomato welcome offer

  1. Set the exact delivery address.
  2. Select the restaurant outlet and intended items.
  3. Open the coupon list from the cart.
  4. Read the minimum value, cap and payment conditions.
  5. Apply the best eligible option and review the final bill.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

The same restaurant can produce different coupon results

Zomato offers can be outlet-specific and personalized. A restaurant brand may participate at one branch but not another, and an offer can disappear when the address or order time changes. Capture the terms before payment if the discount materially affects the purchase.

Why the Zomato welcome coupon may not work

  • The account was not selected for the promotion.
  • The restaurant outlet is excluded.
  • The eligible subtotal is below the minimum.
  • The order falls outside a promotion window.
  • A payment or membership condition is not satisfied.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a universal Zomato welcome code?

No permanent code could be verified for every account. Use the offer shown in the signed-in cart.

Why does my friend see a different offer?

Promotions may vary by account, restaurant, location and time.

Do taxes and fees receive the discount?

Do not assume so. Check which portion of the order the live coupon terms call eligible.

Zomato welcome coupon FAQs

Does Zomato have a first-order coupon code in India?

Zomato runs new-user offers for eligible first orders in India, but the live code, minimum order value and discount cap change by campaign. Open the current offer terms on the Zomato app or website and confirm the discount line in your bag before paying.

What minimum order applies to the Zomato welcome coupon?

The minimum eligible basket for a Zomato welcome coupon is set per campaign and usually counts only coupon-eligible items. If your bag holds excluded styles, restaurants or products, the eligible subtotal can fall below the threshold even when the total looks high enough.

Why is my Zomato coupon code not working?

The usual reasons are: the account or device is not treated as new, the bag contains excluded items, the eligible subtotal is below the minimum, the code was entered on the wrong platform (app vs website), or the campaign ended. Remove excluded items and re-apply the code shown to your signed-in account.

Does the Zomato welcome offer work on the app and the website?

Not always. Some Zomato new-user codes are app-only while others are website-only, and the eligible catalogue can differ between platforms. Check the offer terms for the channel restriction and apply the code on the correct platform.

Can I stack a bank offer with the Zomato welcome coupon?

Sometimes. Apply the welcome coupon first, then proceed to payment and check whether an eligible bank or wallet offer appears on the final order summary. Never count both savings until the checkout displays them together.
