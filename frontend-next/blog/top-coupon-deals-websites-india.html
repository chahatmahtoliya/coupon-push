2. CashKaro: best for cashback stacking

India's best-known cashback portal: click through, shop, and earn a percentage back on top of merchant coupons. Works best when you combine a verified CouponPush coupon guide for the checkout discount with CashKaro for the post-purchase payback. Note cashback can take weeks to confirm and category exclusions apply.

3. GrabOn: biggest coupon catalogue

A large coupon-and-offers directory covering food, fashion, hosting and travel merchants. Catalogue breadth is the strength, but offer terms still need checking per merchant. Before using any GrabOn-listed code for our covered stores, cross-check the fine print in our guides (for example, Hostinger renewal rates or Amazon product-coupon rules).

4. CouponDunia: long-running code directory

One of India's oldest coupon aggregators with merchant pages across categories. Good for discovering which stores have active campaigns; pair it with CouponPush's savings calculator to test whether a listed code beats the auto-applied sale price.

5. DesiDime: best community-spotted deals

A community where shoppers post price drops, loot deals and bank offers in real time. Strong on flash deals, but signal-to-noise varies, so verify any posted code at checkout and read the relevant CouponPush store guide for stacking and cap rules.

6. Zoutons: coupons plus pricing explainers

Combines coupon listings with detailed pricing news and plan breakdowns (hosting, SaaS). A solid second opinion on term-based pricing like web hosting; compare with our Hostinger India price and renewal tables.

How to choose (and stack them)

  1. Read the guide first: open the CouponPush guide for your store (caps, minimums, renewal).
  2. Calculate: run the numbers in the savings calculator including fees and GST.
  3. Grab the live code: check CouponPush offers or the store page (e.g. Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra).
  4. Stack cashback: if a portal like CashKaro covers the merchant, click through it after confirming the coupon works. Never sacrifice a working checkout discount for uncertain cashback.
  5. Screenshot everything: offer terms + order summary, so disputes are easy.
Stacking order matters. Sale price first, then coupon code, then bank/payment offer, then cashback portal. Skipping straight to cashback while ignoring a 75% auto-applied discount (see Hostinger) is the most common way shoppers overpay.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best coupon website in India in 2026?

For explained, guide-backed savings: CouponPush. For cashback stacking: CashKaro. For catalogue breadth: GrabOn. Most smart shoppers use two in combination.

Are coupon codes on deals websites verified?

Quality varies. Prefer listings with visible terms and expiry, and always confirm the discount in the merchant checkout. Our calculator helps you verify the math.

Coupon site vs cashback site: which saves more?

Coupons cut the price now; cashback returns money later. Stack both via CouponPush offers plus a cashback portal where available.
