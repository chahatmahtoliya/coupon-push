Myntra first-order coupon: what new users should know

A clear guide to eligibility, minimum spends and app restrictions—so you can check the real saving before building your basket.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 2 July 2026 · 6 min read

Myntra first-order coupons are intended for accounts that have not completed a previous purchase. The exact discount, minimum order and eligible catalogue can change, so the checkout total—not the headline—is the number that matters.

Quick answer Check that your account is new, your basket meets the minimum value, and every product is part of the eligible catalogue. Some first-order codes only work in the Myntra app.

Who is eligible for a first-order offer?

Eligibility is normally tied to the account, registered phone number and previous order history. Creating another email address may not qualify if the same phone number has already been used for an order.

Requirement What to check
Account status No previous completed order
Minimum basket Read the current coupon terms
Products Only items in the eligible catalogue
Platform Some offers are app-only
Discount cap The maximum saving may be limited

Do not assume that every product displaying a sale price accepts an additional coupon. Brand exclusions and product-level discount caps can reduce or block the extra saving.

How to apply the coupon

  1. Sign in to the account you plan to use for the purchase.
  2. Add eligible items and confirm that you meet the minimum basket value.
  3. Open the bag and proceed to the order summary.
  4. Select Apply Coupon and enter or choose the eligible code.
  5. Check the discount line before selecting your payment method.
Practical check Take a screenshot of the offer terms before ordering. It gives you a useful record if the discount or eligibility changes later.

Why a Myntra coupon may not work

A failed code does not always mean it has expired. The most common causes are:

  • The account has placed an earlier order.
  • The basket is below the required amount.
  • One or more products are excluded.
  • The maximum number of redemptions has been reached.
  • The code is restricted to the app or a specific payment method.

Remove excluded products and try the code again before increasing your basket. Adding unnecessary items just to cross a threshold can erase the benefit of the coupon.

Ways to save more

Compare the final payable amount

Look beyond the displayed percentage. Delivery charges, convenience fees and the discount cap all affect the final saving.

Check eligible bank offers

Some payment offers can apply separately from a store coupon. Read the bank terms carefully because card type, transaction value and offer dates matter.
