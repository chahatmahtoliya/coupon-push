How the estimate works

The financed amount is the product price minus the upfront EMI discount and down payment. For a monthly rate r and n installments, EMI = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n). The monthly rate is the annual percentage divided by 1,200. At 0% interest, EMI is simply the financed amount divided by the number of months. This follows the reducing-balance approach described in Axis Bank’s EMI formula.

Payments are assumed to occur monthly, with the first payment after one month. Calculations retain precision internally and display two decimals. A lender’s rounding, first-period interest, billing dates or tax timing may produce a different schedule. Flat-rate loans, late fees, foreclosure and other unentered charges are not included.

Is no-cost EMI always cheaper?

Compare the offer’s full repayment cost with the price you can pay today. If the lender charges interest and the merchant gives an upfront subsidy, enter the quoted interest rate and that subsidy in “Upfront EMI discount.” Do not also set the rate to zero unless the lender actually charges 0%.

Processing fees and applicable taxes can still affect the total. For example, HDFC Bank’s EasyEMI terms describe processing charges and tax billing. The calculator does not fetch any bank’s current rates: use your own offer and lender terms. The difference shown is a nominal cost comparison, not a recommendation to borrow.
