How these comparisons are maintained

Each listing links to a merchant page and shows when its advertised wording was checked. Source matching does not establish that a code works at checkout, that offers combine, or that an account qualifies.

Listings whose wording cannot be found or whose source checks have aged out are withheld from the comparisons. We do not infer a promotion’s end date when the reviewed banner does not state one.

Compare the final amount payable for the same basket. Gift values, future rewards and headline percentages are different benefits; they should not be added together as cash savings.
