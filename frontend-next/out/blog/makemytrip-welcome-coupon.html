MakeMyTrip Welcome Coupon: First-Booking Offer Rules

MakeMyTrip uses WELCOMEMMT across selected new-user campaigns, but the discount differs by trains, cabs, holidays and other booking types.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 3 July 2026 · 7 min read
On this page Quick answer Offer types Eligibility How to apply Why it may fail FAQs

Searching for a MakeMyTrip welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerWELCOMEMMT is not one universal discount for every booking. MakeMyTrip's current campaign page describes different benefits and conditions by line of business. Select the booking type first, then read its minimum value, route, cap and cancellation rules.

MakeMyTrip welcome-offer snapshot

Offer route Code or type What to verify
Train WELCOMEMMT First-ticket benefit shown for eligible bookings
Outstation cab WELCOMEMMT Select routes and a stated minimum booking value
Holiday WELCOMEMMT Percentage benefit with a category-specific cap

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The logged-in customer must be new to the relevant booking type.
  • The route, property or package must participate.
  • The base fare or booking value must meet the stated minimum.
  • The booking channel and travel dates must satisfy the campaign.

How to apply the MakeMyTrip welcome offer

  1. Choose flights, hotels, trains, cabs or holidays before comparing the offer.
  2. Enter trip details and select an eligible result.
  3. Review Booking and enter WELCOMEMMT in the e-coupon field.
  4. Check whether the discount applies to base price or total price.
  5. Read cancellation deductions before completing payment.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

First booking can mean first booking in a category

A customer who has booked a flight may still be new to outstation cabs, depending on the campaign definition. Conversely, a general new account does not guarantee eligibility for every WELCOMEMMT promotion. Treat each booking category's terms separately.

Why the MakeMyTrip welcome coupon may not work

  • The customer has already booked that service type.
  • The selected route or package is excluded.
  • The eligible base value is below the minimum.
  • Another e-coupon is already applied.
  • The discount is being calculated against taxes and fees that the offer excludes.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

What does WELCOMEMMT cover?

Coverage depends on the specific campaign and booking category. Read the terms shown for the selected service.

Can it be combined with another MakeMyTrip coupon?

MakeMyTrip's campaign terms commonly state that it cannot be clubbed with another offer.

What happens after cancellation?

The discount can be deducted from the refund and the normal cancellation charge may still apply.
