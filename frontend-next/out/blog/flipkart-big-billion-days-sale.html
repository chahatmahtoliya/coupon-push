Bank offers (confirmed)

Flipkart's microsite confirms up to 10% instant discount with Axis Bank and ICICI Bank cards. Before counting it:

  • Check the minimum transaction value — small baskets may not qualify.
  • Check the per-card cap — 10% on a flagship can hit the ceiling fast.
  • Check EMI conditions — no-cost EMI tenures and eligible cards are restricted.
  • Read the full bank-offer maths in our Diwali bank offers guide.

Big Billion Days FAQs

When does Flipkart Big Billion Days 2026 start?

October 9 for all customers, with early access from October 8 for Plus, Black and Flipkart Credit Card members.

Which bank offers work in Big Billion Days 2026?

Up to 10% instant discount on Axis Bank and ICICI Bank cards, per Flipkart's microsite. Minimums, caps and EMI terms apply — confirm on the payment page.
