Axis and ICICI card offers

Flipkart's Big Billion Days page advertises up to 10% instant discount on eligible Axis Bank and ICICI Bank card transactions. Its early-bird page also mentions selected Axis and ICICI card offers. Neither headline guarantees 10% on every basket: use the current payment page for the eligible card, minimum, cap and final discount.

Big Billion Days FAQs

When does Flipkart Big Billion Days 2026 start?

Flipkart confirms October 8 early access in its announcement. Its sale page lists general public access from October 9, 2026.

Which bank offers work in BBD 2026?

Flipkart's sale page advertises up to 10% on eligible Axis and ICICI Bank card transactions. Check the live payment page for the cap, minimum and exclusions.

Do coupons stack with sale prices?

Only when the live cart accepts the coupon and the final order summary shows both discounts. Never assume an older code still applies.

Is early access worth it?

It can matter for a product with limited stock, but that is a buying decision—not a promise that every item will be cheaper early.

BBD vs Great Indian Festival: which is cheaper?

Neither is always cheaper. Compare the exact variant, seller, delivery, exchange and final payment total with the comparison guide.
