Zepto Welcome Coupon: New-User Offer Explained

Zepto welcome promotions are typically surfaced inside the app and can change by account, service area, basket and campaign availability.

CouponPush Editorial · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

Last verified: 16 Sep 2026 (IST). Terms change per campaign — confirm the discount line at checkout.

Searching for a Zepto welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerCheck the new account's Zepto app after setting the delivery location. A single permanent public welcome code could not be verified, so do not plan a basket around an amount shown on an old coupon page.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The account must be marked eligible.
  • The delivery address must be inside the campaign area.
  • The basket must contain participating products.
  • Any minimum value must remain satisfied after unavailable items are removed.

How to apply the Zepto welcome offer

  1. Register and set the correct address.
  2. Open the welcome banner or coupon area.
  3. Build the intended basket without relying on excluded items.
  4. Apply the available offer and check its cap.
  5. Review handling, delivery and surge charges before payment.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

Fast-changing inventory can change coupon eligibility

Quick-commerce baskets can change when an item goes out of stock or is substituted. If that product contributed to a coupon threshold, the saving may disappear. Recheck the order summary immediately before paying.

Why the Zepto welcome coupon may not work

  • The offer is not active for the account.
  • The service location has different promotions.
  • An eligible item went out of stock.
  • Fees do not count toward the minimum basket.
  • The campaign allocation ended.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a permanent Zepto welcome code?

No permanent public code could be verified. Use the offer displayed in the current app session.

Why did the discount disappear?

Inventory changes, basket thresholds, account targeting or campaign expiry can remove it.

Do delivery and handling charges count?

Do not assume they count toward the eligible merchandise threshold. Read the live terms.

Zepto welcome coupon FAQs

Does Zepto have a first-order coupon code in India?

Zepto runs new-user offers for eligible first orders in India, but the live code, minimum order value and discount cap change by campaign. Open the current offer terms on the Zepto app or website and confirm the discount line in your bag before paying.

What minimum order applies to the Zepto welcome coupon?

The minimum eligible basket for a Zepto welcome coupon is set per campaign and usually counts only coupon-eligible items. If your bag holds excluded styles, restaurants or products, the eligible subtotal can fall below the threshold even when the total looks high enough.

Why is my Zepto coupon code not working?

The usual reasons are: the account or device is not treated as new, the bag contains excluded items, the eligible subtotal is below the minimum, the code was entered on the wrong platform (app vs website), or the campaign ended. Remove excluded items and re-apply the code shown to your signed-in account.

Does the Zepto welcome offer work on the app and the website?

Not always. Some Zepto new-user codes are app-only while others are website-only, and the eligible catalogue can differ between platforms. Check the offer terms for the channel restriction and apply the code on the correct platform.

Can I stack a bank offer with the Zepto welcome coupon?

Sometimes. Apply the welcome coupon first, then proceed to payment and check whether an eligible bank or wallet offer appears on the final order summary. Never count both savings until the checkout displays them together.
