Minimum-cap maths (worked examples)

Formula: saving = min(eligible amount × rate, cap), valid only above the minimum transaction value.

  • Rs. 8,000 phone accessory basket at 10% up to Rs. 1,000 → saves Rs. 800 (full 10%).
  • Rs. 50,000 flagship at 10% up to Rs. 1,000 → saves Rs. 1,000 — effectively 2%. A rival platform with 10% up to Rs. 2,000 wins here.
  • Rs. 4,999 basket with a Rs. 5,000 minimum → saves Rs. 0. Adding a Rs. 200 filler you don't need to "unlock" Rs. 500 still wastes money if the filler has no use.

EMI traps

  • No-cost EMI sometimes removes the bank discount or shortens eligible tenures — compare EMI total vs full-payment total.
  • Debit-card EMI and credit-card EMI carry different eligibility; check your card variant.
  • Processing fees and GST on interest can erase small discounts — read the loan summary, not the banner.

Payment-page checklist

  1. Apply sale price + store coupon first, then select the eligible card.
  2. Confirm the instant-discount line appears with the correct amount.
  3. Check minimum, cap, once-per-card usage and category exclusions.
  4. Run the final total in the savings calculator before the OTP.
Stacking order: sale price → store coupon → bank offer. See the full mechanics in the Diwali sale guide and per-store rules in the Flipkart guide and Amazon guide.

Bank offer FAQs

Which bank offers are confirmed for Diwali 2026?

BBD: up to 10% instant discount on Axis and ICICI cards. GIF: partner awaited. Only microsite-published terms count.

How does a 10% discount with a cap work?

10% of the eligible amount up to the cap — e.g. 10% up to Rs. 1,000 saves at most Rs. 1,000 regardless of basket size.

Do bank offers apply on EMI?

Sometimes, with tenure and card restrictions. Compare EMI totals against full payment — "no-cost" can hide costs.

Can bank offers stack with coupons?

Usually yes. Confirm sale price, coupon and bank lines together in the final summary.

Why did my bank offer not apply?

Below-minimum basket, cap reached, wrong card variant, EMI mismatch, once-per-card used, or excluded category.
