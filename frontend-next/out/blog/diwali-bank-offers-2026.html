Minimum-cap maths

Use saving = min(eligible amount × rate, cap), and only when the transaction meets the published minimum. These are illustrations, not 2026 merchant terms:

  • ₹8,000 basket at 10% up to ₹1,000 → ₹800 saving.
  • ₹50,000 basket at 10% up to ₹1,000 → ₹1,000 saving, or an effective 2%.
  • ₹4,999 basket with a ₹5,000 minimum → ₹0 bank saving until the minimum is met.

EMI and payment checks

  • Confirm whether the offer covers credit, debit, EMI or only a specified tenure.
  • Compare the EMI total with full payment; processing fees and GST can change the result.
  • Check whether a coupon disappears when the bank offer is selected.

Payment-page checklist

  1. Apply the sale price and any accepted store coupon.
  2. Select the eligible card or EMI method shown by the merchant.
  3. Confirm the discount line, minimum, cap, exclusions and one-time limits.
  4. Compare delivery, exchange, warranty and EMI totals before the OTP.
  5. Run the final figure through the savings calculator.
Rule: a bank offer is confirmed only when the official sale page or payment step states it. Old sale pages and social screenshots are historical context.

Bank offer FAQs

Which bank offers are confirmed for 2026?

Amazon has announced its SBI credit/debit card and EMI offer. Flipkart's 2026 sale page advertises up to 10% on eligible Axis and ICICI Bank card transactions. Check each payment page for the exact minimum and cap.

How does a capped discount work?

Multiply the eligible amount by the rate, then stop at the cap. A larger basket does not create savings above that cap.

Do bank offers apply on EMI?

Only when the live terms include the selected EMI type and tenure. Compare the loan summary with full payment.

Can bank offers stack with coupons?

Sometimes. Apply the coupon, select the payment method and use the final order summary as the answer.

Why did a bank offer not apply?

Common causes are a missed minimum, wrong card or tenure, a reached cap, an excluded item, or a used once-per-card limit.
