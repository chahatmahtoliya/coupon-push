Cetaphil Gentle vs Oily Skin Cleanser: Which Fits You?

The two bottles serve different skin-type needs. Here is what Cetaphil India's product pages actually say.

Choosing between Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser and Oily Skin Cleanser starts with the skin type each product targets. We compared Cetaphil India's Gentle Skin Cleanser listing with its Oily Skin Cleanser listing. The table reflects those pages as reviewed on 24 Sep 2026.

Quick answerChoose the Gentle cleanser if you have dry to normal sensitive skin and want a creamy, fragrance-free formula. Consider the Oily cleanser if you have combination to oily sensitive skin and prefer a low-lather gel aimed at removing excess oil. Personal tolerance still matters.

The ingredient difference that matters most

Both current India lists include glycerin, niacinamide and panthenol. The Gentle cleanser is labelled fragrance free. The Oily cleanser lists parfum and named fragrance components including linalool. If fragrance avoidance is a key requirement for you, that difference may be more useful than the marketing name alone.

The Oily cleanser also lists cleansing agents such as cocamidopropyl betaine and disodium laureth sulfosuccinate; its gel format and excess-oil emphasis are consistent with the brand's positioning. An ingredient list does not tell us how every face will feel after use, so this is a formula comparison rather than a performance ranking.

Which one should you pick?

  • Your skin often feels dry or tight after washing: start by reading the Gentle cleanser's pack and our source-based Gentle Skin Cleanser review.
  • You want to remove noticeable excess oil: the Oily cleanser is the product Cetaphil positions for that job.
  • You avoid perfume: the current Gentle listing is the clearer fit because the Oily ingredient list includes fragrance.
  • Your skin is both oily and reactive: compare the full ingredients with your known triggers and test cautiously; neither label guarantees personal tolerance.

If you use other products that leave skin dry, the gentler-feeling option may matter more than a strong-clean feel. This is an inference from the products' stated texture and positioning, not a clinical recommendation.
