Electronics deal FAQs

Is Diwali sale the best time to buy a laptop?

Usually yes for mainstream configurations — festival price plus exchange plus bank offer combine into the year's lowest all-in totals. Baseline your exact specs first.

Do exchange offers apply on laptops and TVs?

Smaller and condition-dependent than phones. Get the inspection quote in writing and compare with selling separately.

Is no-cost EMI actually free?

Not always — compare EMI totals with full payment and read the loan summary for removed discounts and fees.

Should I buy extended warranty in the sale?

For TVs and large appliances, bundled warranty years can outweigh a lower bare price elsewhere. Price it separately.
