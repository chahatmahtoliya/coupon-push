Fashion & beauty first orders

AJIO publishes separate website and app codes, Myntra leans app-only for first orders, and Nykaa splits flat vs percentage new-user options with brand exclusions. Read the AJIO guide, Myntra guide and Nykaa guide — caps and eligible styles differ by store, so a higher headline percentage can pay less.

Food & grocery first orders

Swiggy and Zomato offers change by restaurant and delivery location; Blinkit, Zepto and BigBasket offers change by basket and serviceability. Check the Swiggy guide, Zomato guide, Blinkit guide, Zepto guide and BigBasket guide — the offer shown to one account or address may not appear for another.

Shopping & travel first orders

Amazon and Flipkart rarely offer one universal first-order code; savings come from product-level coupons plus payment offers. FirstCry restricts by category and household; MakeMyTrip splits by booking type. See the Amazon guide, Flipkart guide, Meesho guide, FirstCry guide and MakeMyTrip guide.

How to choose (and verify) in 4 steps

  1. Open the store guide above and note the minimum, cap and platform rule.
  2. Build your basket from eligible items only, then apply the code or coupon.
  3. Run the total through the savings calculator including delivery and fees.

First-order coupon FAQs

Which first-order coupon is best in India?

There is no single best coupon. Compare the final payable amount for your own basket using the store guide above — a higher percentage with a low cap often loses to a smaller percentage with no cap.

Do first-order coupons need a code?

Sometimes. AJIO publishes codes; Amazon and Flipkart mostly auto-apply product and payment offers. Use the offer shown to your signed-in account, not an old code from another blog.

Why do new-user coupons fail on the first order?

Usually: not treated as a new account, excluded items in the bag, eligible subtotal below the minimum, wrong platform (app vs website), or an ended campaign.

Can I use a first-order coupon with a bank offer?

Sometimes. Apply the store coupon first, then check at payment whether a bank offer appears. Count both only when the order summary shows them together.
