AJIO Welcome Coupon: First-Order Eligibility and Limits

A checkout-first guide to AJIO's website and app welcome codes, eligible products and the limits that can reduce the advertised saving.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

Last verified: 16 Sep 2026 (IST). Terms change per campaign — confirm the discount line at checkout.

On this page Quick answer Offer types Eligibility How to apply Why it may fail FAQs

Searching for a AJIO welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerAJIO currently lists FIRSTBUY for its website and FIRSTAPP for its mobile apps. Its official offers page advertises an extra 30% to 33% for eligible new users, but the applicable products, minimum purchase and maximum saving must be checked in the bag.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The AJIO account must qualify as a new customer.
  • Use FIRSTBUY on the website or FIRSTAPP in the app.
  • Every item contributing to the threshold must accept the selected coupon.
  • Meet the minimum eligible merchandise value shown in the coupon terms.

How to apply the AJIO welcome offer

  1. Sign in before building the final bag.
  2. Open each product page and check the coupons shown below its price.
  3. Add eligible styles and open Apply Coupon in the bag.
  4. Use the platform-appropriate welcome code.
  5. Confirm the coupon line and final payable amount before choosing payment.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

Why AJIO's displayed percentage can be misleading

AJIO applies coupons only to selected merchandise and can impose a maximum discount. A heavily discounted item may contribute less additional coupon value than expected. Compare the final payable amount, not the headline percentage, and remove an excluded item before increasing the basket.

Why the AJIO welcome coupon may not work

  • FIRSTBUY is being entered in the app, or FIRSTAPP on the website.
  • The bag contains styles that do not participate in the coupon.
  • The eligible portion of the bag is below the minimum purchase.
  • The account or device is not treated as a new customer.
  • The offer changed after it was first displayed.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Does AJIO have a welcome coupon?

AJIO currently publishes separate new-user codes for its website and mobile apps. Check the live offer terms because the eligible catalogue and discount cap can change.

Can the AJIO welcome coupon apply to sale products?

It may apply to selected sale products, but a sale label alone does not establish coupon eligibility. Check the offers listed on the product page.

Can I use a bank offer too?

Proceed to payment after applying the coupon and check whether an eligible bank offer appears. Do not count both savings until the order summary displays them.

AJIO welcome coupon FAQs

Does AJIO have a first-order coupon code in India?

AJIO runs new-user offers for eligible first orders in India, but the live code, minimum order value and discount cap change by campaign. Open the current offer terms on the AJIO app or website and confirm the discount line in your bag before paying.

What minimum order applies to the AJIO welcome coupon?

The minimum eligible basket for a AJIO welcome coupon is set per campaign and usually counts only coupon-eligible items. If your bag holds excluded styles, restaurants or products, the eligible subtotal can fall below the threshold even when the total looks high enough.

Why is my AJIO coupon code not working?

The usual reasons are: the account or device is not treated as new, the bag contains excluded items, the eligible subtotal is below the minimum, the code was entered on the wrong platform (app vs website), or the campaign ended. Remove excluded items and re-apply the code shown to your signed-in account.

Does the AJIO welcome offer work on the app and the website?

Not always. Some AJIO new-user codes are app-only while others are website-only, and the eligible catalogue can differ between platforms. Check the offer terms for the channel restriction and apply the code on the correct platform.

Can I stack a bank offer with the AJIO welcome coupon?

Sometimes. Apply the welcome coupon first, then proceed to payment and check whether an eligible bank or wallet offer appears on the final order summary. Never count both savings until the checkout displays them together.
