Amazon Great Indian Festival Sale 2026: Date, Offers & Prep

Official October 8 start, published SBI offer and Prime benefit, plus the details Amazon has not yet specified.

Amazon has officially announced that its Great Indian Festival 2026 starts on October 8. The announcement also names a 10% instant discount on eligible SBI credit and debit card and EMI transactions, plus up to 10% extra for Prime members. Prime benefits and timing

Amazon's 2026 announcement confirms up to 10% extra for Prime members, but it does not publish a separate Prime early-access time. Do not treat past-year early-access patterns as a 2026 promise. Check the Amazon event page and your product page when the sale opens.

Prep checklist

  1. Shortlist the exact model, size, colour, storage and seller you would actually buy.
  2. Record today's price and delivery promise so a future badge has a real baseline.
  3. Keep an eligible SBI card or EMI option ready, then verify the exact minimum, cap and exclusions on Amazon's payment page.
  4. Check exchange value, warranty, delivery and return terms separately from the headline discount.
  5. Compare the exact Amazon total with the Flipkart BBD hub and our BBD vs GIF comparison.
  6. Use the savings calculator only after the live order summary shows every discount.

Great Indian Festival FAQs

When does Amazon Great Indian Festival 2026 start?

Amazon says the Great Indian Festival starts on October 8, 2026. Product-level start times, end dates and offer limits can vary, so check the live event and checkout.

Do Prime members get early access?

Amazon's 2026 announcement confirms up to 10% extra for Prime members but does not state a separate early-access window. Treat any earlier timing as unconfirmed until Amazon publishes it.

Which bank offer is confirmed?

Amazon has announced a 10% instant discount on eligible SBI credit and debit card and EMI transactions. Minimums, caps and product exclusions must be checked on the live offer.

Should I buy on Amazon or wait for BBD?

Both brands have published October 8 as an important 2026 date: Amazon's GIF starts then, while Flipkart says BBD early access begins then. Compare the exact live total per variant with our comparison guide.

How should I prepare?

Shortlist variants, record current prices, check seller and return terms, and keep an eligible payment method ready.
