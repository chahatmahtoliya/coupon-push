BigBasket Welcome Coupon: First-Purchase Offer Guide

BigBasket offers and inventory vary by city. An old first-order landing page still appears in search, but its published MYFIRST200 campaign expired in April 2021.

CP CouponPush Editorial · Updated 3 July 2026 · 7 min read
On this page Quick answer Offer types Eligibility How to apply Why it may fail FAQs

Searching for a BigBasket welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerDo not treat MYFIRST200 as a current welcome code: BigBasket's own archived page gives that campaign an April 2021 end date. Set your city, sign in and check today's BB offers or payment promotions instead.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The offer must be active in the selected city.
  • The account and payment method must qualify.
  • Excluded grocery categories must not be counted toward the threshold.
  • The order must remain eligible after substitutions or cancellations.

How to apply the BigBasket welcome offer

  1. Set the correct city and delivery address.
  2. Open BB offers from the signed-in account.
  3. Ignore codes whose official validity has ended.
  4. Check category exclusions and eligible basket value.
  5. Distinguish instant discount from wallet cashback before ordering.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

Why expired BigBasket codes continue to appear

Search engines can retain old promotional landing pages long after a campaign ends. The presence of an official BigBasket URL does not make its code current. Always read the validity date and check the active account offer.

Why the BigBasket welcome coupon may not work

  • The code belongs to an expired campaign.
  • The offer is unavailable in the selected city.
  • Excluded staples reduce the eligible total.
  • The payment partner's new-user rule is not met.
  • A substituted or cancelled item drops the basket below the threshold.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Is MYFIRST200 still a valid BigBasket welcome coupon?

The official landing page states that campaign ended in April 2021, so it should not be presented as current.

Why does location matter?

BigBasket says product availability, prices and promotions can vary by city.

Is cashback an immediate discount?

Not necessarily. A payment cashback campaign may credit a wallet after the order instead of reducing checkout.
