Diwali Fashion Sale Guide 2026: Myntra, AJIO, Nykaa Compared

Exclusions, caps and size timing: how fashion sale savings are really decided across the three big platforms.

Fashion "up to 80% off" banners hide the mechanics that decide your total: brand exclusions, coupon caps and size availability. Myntra, AJIO and Nykaa run different sale engines — here's how each one behaves.

Quick answer AJIO: platform codes (website vs app) with style exclusions. Myntra: brand-heavy coupon layering, often app-first. Nykaa: flat-vs-percentage choice with brand exclusions. Shortlist with size filters now; compare final payable totals per basket.

Three sale traps

  • The exclusion trap: premium and luxe brands routinely sit outside extra coupons. Check listed offers on each product page.
  • The cap trap: a 30%-off coupon capped at Rs. 500 saves less than a flat Rs. 750 code on the same basket.
  • The size trap: popular sizes vanish in 24–48 hours. High-intent occasion wear goes first; basics can wait for later waves.

Comparison method

  1. Build identical baskets (same styles, sizes) on each platform.
  2. Apply the platform code and note the discount line plus delivery fees.
  3. Check return windows — non-returnable occasion wear is a risk multiplier.
  4. Run totals through the savings calculator and buy per basket, not per platform.
First order + sale: welcome codes often pause on sale catalogue. Check the first-order hub for your store before counting a new-user code.
