Blinkit Welcome Coupon: Minimum Order and Eligibility

Blinkit promotions can be targeted by account, product category and delivery area. The app's live offer terms determine the eligible customer and basket.

CouponPush Editorial · Updated 16 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

Last verified: 16 Sep 2026 (IST). Terms change per campaign — confirm the discount line at checkout.

Searching for a Blinkit welcome coupon often produces old codes and offers written for a different account or platform. This guide separates confirmed offer mechanics from conditions that must be checked at checkout.

Quick answerNo permanent public welcome code should be assumed for every Blinkit user. Check the signed-in app after setting the delivery address. Blinkit's promotional terms allow offers to carry minimum-order or category conditions and to be withdrawn.

Who is eligible?

Welcome does not always mean every newly created account. The current promotion decides which user, basket and purchase qualify.

  • The account must be named or selected as eligible.
  • The delivery location must be served by the campaign.
  • The basket must meet product or category conditions.
  • The completed order must continue to satisfy the terms after cancellations.

How to apply the Blinkit welcome offer

  1. Set the final delivery address.
  2. Open the account's coupons or promotional banner.
  3. Add eligible products and review the basket threshold.
  4. Check whether the benefit is instant discount or later cashback.
  5. Confirm the final total and retain the offer terms.
Practical checkTake a screenshot of the offer terms and final order summary. Promotions can change, and the record helps if the displayed benefit does not track correctly.

Instant discount and cashback are not interchangeable

A cashback promotion may credit value after delivery rather than reduce today's payment. Blinkit's terms also allow a minimum-value or category offer to be cancelled when qualifying products are cancelled, returned or refunded.

Why the Blinkit welcome coupon may not work

  • The delivery area is not included.
  • The eligible products fall below the minimum value.
  • The account is outside the targeted group.
  • A qualifying item became unavailable.
  • The promotion was revoked or exhausted.

Do not add an unwanted item only to cross a threshold until you compare its cost with the additional saving. If the offer still fails, remove excluded items, recheck the eligible subtotal and use the offer displayed to the signed-in account.

Frequently asked questions

Does Blinkit give every new user the same coupon?

No universal offer could be verified. Promotions can be targeted and location-dependent.

When will cashback appear?

The timing depends on the specific campaign; Blinkit's terms say some promotional cashback can take longer than an instant discount.

What happens if an eligible item is cancelled?

The promotional benefit may be cancelled if the order no longer satisfies its product or minimum-value conditions.

Blinkit welcome coupon FAQs

Does Blinkit have a first-order coupon code in India?

Blinkit runs new-user offers for eligible first orders in India, but the live code, minimum order value and discount cap change by campaign. Open the current offer terms on the Blinkit app or website and confirm the discount line in your bag before paying.

What minimum order applies to the Blinkit welcome coupon?

The minimum eligible basket for a Blinkit welcome coupon is set per campaign and usually counts only coupon-eligible items. If your bag holds excluded styles, restaurants or products, the eligible subtotal can fall below the threshold even when the total looks high enough.

Why is my Blinkit coupon code not working?

The usual reasons are: the account or device is not treated as new, the bag contains excluded items, the eligible subtotal is below the minimum, the code was entered on the wrong platform (app vs website), or the campaign ended. Remove excluded items and re-apply the code shown to your signed-in account.

Does the Blinkit welcome offer work on the app and the website?

Not always. Some Blinkit new-user codes are app-only while others are website-only, and the eligible catalogue can differ between platforms. Check the offer terms for the channel restriction and apply the code on the correct platform.

Can I stack a bank offer with the Blinkit welcome coupon?

Sometimes. Apply the welcome coupon first, then proceed to payment and check whether an eligible bank or wallet offer appears on the final order summary. Never count both savings until the checkout displays them together.
