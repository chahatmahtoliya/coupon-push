Set your baseline this week

  1. Fix the exact variant: model, storage, colour, seller.
  2. Record today's price on both Flipkart and Amazon — screenshot it.
  3. Note the current exchange quote for your old phone and the card you will pay with.
  4. When the sale opens, compute: baseline − sale discount − coupon − bank saving − exchange = true saving.
