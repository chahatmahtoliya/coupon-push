<h2>Why a Promo Code May Not Work</h2>
<p><strong>Code shows invalid:</strong> The offer has expired or hit its usage limit. Try a different code from this page.</p>
<p><strong>Discount not applied:</strong> Your cart value is below the minimum required. Add more items to meet the threshold.</p>
<p><strong>Code valid but no discount showing:</strong> The product may be in an excluded category. Check the offer terms for eligible categories before applying.</p>
<p><strong>Bank offer not reflecting:</strong> You may have selected the wrong card or the card is not saved on your account. Use the exact bank card mentioned in the offer.</p>
<p><strong>Promo code field not visible:</strong> You may be on an older version of the Amazon app. Update the app or switch to amazon.in on a browser.</p>
