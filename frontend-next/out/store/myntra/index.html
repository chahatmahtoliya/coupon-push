About Myntra

Myntra is India's largest online fashion and lifestyle marketplace, founded in 2007 and now owned by Flipkart, which is a subsidiary of Walmart. Headquartered in Bengaluru, Myntra hosts over 6,000 brands and more than 1 million products across clothing, footwear, accessories, beauty, and home decor. It serves customers across 19,000+ pin codes in India and processes millions of orders every month, making it the go-to destination for fashion shopping online in India.


Myntra caters to men, women, and kids across every price point, from affordable everyday basics to premium international labels. The platform features both Indian brands like Biba, W, and FabIndia alongside global names like H&M, Zara, Nike, Puma, Adidas, Levi's, and Mango. Whether you are shopping for ethnic wear, western casuals, activewear, or formal office attire, Myntra covers the full range.

Myntra Product Categories



Women's Fashion


Myntra's women's section is the largest category on the platform. How to Use a Myntra Coupon Code

Myntra coupon codes work differently on the app versus the website, and it is important to know the correct steps to make sure your discount applies successfully before you place the order.


How to Add Coupon in Myntra App



Step 1: Open the Myntra app on your phone and browse for the product you want to buy.

Step 2: Add the product to your bag by tapping the Add to Bag button on the product page.

Step 3: Tap the bag icon at the bottom right of the screen to open your cart.

Step 4: Review your items and tap Proceed to Buy.

Step 5: On the order summary page, look for the field that says Have a Coupon Code? or Apply Coupon. Tap on it.

Step 6: A list of available coupons will appear. You can either select one from the list or manually type or paste the coupon code you copied from CouponPush.

Step 7: Tap Apply. The discount will reflect immediately in your order total on the same screen.

Step 8: Select your delivery address and payment method, then place the order.

Note: Most Myntra promo codes are valid on the app only. If a code is not working on the website, switch to the Myntra app and try again.

How to Apply Coupon in Myntra Website



Step 1: Visit myntra.com and log into your account.

Step 2: Add your chosen items to the bag and click on the bag icon at the top right.

Step 3: Review your cart and click Proceed to Buy.

Step 4: On the checkout page, scroll to the section that says Apply Coupon Code.

Step 5: Type or paste the promo code from CouponPush into the field and click Apply.

Step 6: Confirm the discounted price appears in the order summary before selecting your payment method.

Step 7: Complete your payment and place the order.

How to Use Myntra Coupon for First Order



New Myntra users get access to exclusive first-order coupon codes that give a flat discount of Rs.200 to Rs.500 on a minimum purchase. To use a first-order coupon, create a new Myntra account with your mobile number or email, add products to your cart that meet the minimum order value stated in the offer, and apply the first-order promo code at checkout. First-order codes are valid on the Myntra app only and are linked to your registered mobile number, so they cannot be reused on a second account with the same number.

Best Times to Shop on Myntra with Coupons



End of Reason Sale (EORS): Myntra's flagship twice-yearly sale held in June and December. Discounts go up to 80 percent across all categories with additional bank offers and exclusive app coupon codes. This is the best time of year to shop on Myntra.

Pink Friday Sale: Myntra's answer to Black Friday, held in November. Deep discounts on beauty, fashion, and footwear with extra flat-off coupon codes for app users.

Myntra Diwali Sale: Held in October, this sale focuses on festive and ethnic wear with up to 70 percent off on sherwanis, lehengas, and kurtis, plus extra coupon codes on wedding wear collections.

Big Fashion Festival: Usually held around Republic Day in January. Up to 80 percent off with bank coupon offers giving an additional 10 percent instant discount.

Monthly Insider Offers: Myntra Insider members receive exclusive discount codes on the 28th of every month regardless of any ongoing sale. These are small but consistent savings for regular shoppers.

Tips to Save More on Myntra



Stack a promo code with a bank offer. Myntra allows you to apply one promo code and one bank-specific discount on the same order. This is the most effective way to maximize savings, particularly during sale events when both types of discounts are active simultaneously.

Download the Myntra app for app-exclusive codes. Most high-value Myntra coupon codes including first-order discounts are only valid on the app. Shopping through the website will not give you access to these codes.

Join Myntra Insider for free. The Insider program costs nothing and gives you early sale access, monthly discount codes, and exclusive product drops that are not available to regular users.

Check your Myntra Credit balance before checkout. Myntra Credit is earned through referrals, gift cards, and refunds. It can be applied at checkout like a coupon to get an additional reduction on your order total.

Buy combos and sets instead of individual items. Myntra regularly offers bundle deals where buying two or three items together gives a larger combined discount than buying each product separately. Look for Buy 2 Get 1 or Buy 3 Get Extra 30% Off deals on the offers page.
