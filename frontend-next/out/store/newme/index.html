NEWME Coupon Code FAQs

Does a NEWME coupon apply to sale items?

Check the specific promotion and your cart. Some offers exclude already-discounted styles or selected collections; do not assume a code is an extra discount on every sale item.

Can I use a NEWME first-order code on the website?

Confirm the channel in the offer terms. An app-only code may not work on the website, and a welcome offer may be tied to an eligible account.

NEWME offer checklist

18 offers are listed, including 5 codes and 13 deals. Listed availability is not a guarantee that an offer applies to your account.

  • Eligible styles and sizesCheck the code against the exact style, colour and size in your basket. An offer for selected products may exclude other sale items.
  • App and first-order rulesConfirm whether the offer is app-only or requires a first purchase. Account eligibility and minimum spend can change the discount.
  • Total order costInclude shipping and payment fees when comparing codes. Review returns and refund terms separately before buying extra items to reach a discount threshold.

How to use a NEWME coupon code

Choose a listed offer and review its conditions. Add eligible products on NEWME, enter the code if one is required, and confirm the discount in your order summary. An automatic deal does not need a code.

Compare the final payable amount, including delivery and payment fees, for the same products and quantities. If a code fails, check minimum spend, exclusions, expiry, account eligibility and app or website restrictions. Do not assume two offers can be combined.
