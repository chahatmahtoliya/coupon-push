About Cetaphil

What is Cetaphil?


Cetaphil is a dermatologist-recommended skincare brand founded in 1947 by a pharmacist in Texas, United States. What started as a simple, gentle cleanser formula has grown into one of the world's most trusted skincare brands, now owned by Galderma, a global leader in skin health science. Today, Cetaphil is available in over 70 countries and is used daily by millions of people dealing with sensitive, dry, acne-prone, or compromised skin.

In India, Cetaphil has built a loyal following among dermatologists, skincare enthusiasts, and everyday consumers who want effective skincare without unnecessary ingredients. The brand's philosophy has always been simple: clinically tested formulas that work without irritating the skin. Every Cetaphil product is developed in collaboration with dermatologists and backed by over 75 years of skin science research.

Cetaphil products in India are available on the official website cetaphil.in as well as on Amazon, Nykaa, Flipkart, Firstcry, and Netmeds. The brand covers a wide range of skin concerns from everyday cleansing and moisturizing to targeted treatments for oily skin, baby care, and brightening.

What makes Cetaphil stand out from other skincare brands is its commitment to skin barrier protection. All Cetaphil formulas are fragrance-free, pH-balanced, non-comedogenic, and hypoallergenic. This means they are safe for daily use across all skin types, including the most sensitive and reactive skin. If your skin is easily triggered by new products, Cetaphil is consistently one of the safest starting points recommended by skin specialists.

Is Cetaphil a Good Face Wash?


Yes, and it is one of the most consistently recommended face washes by dermatologists in India for everyday use. The reason Cetaphil stands apart from most face washes in the market is its non-foaming, soap-free formulation. Most face washes rely on sulphates and harsh surfactants to create lather, which effectively removes dirt but also strips away the skin's natural oils and disrupts its pH balance. This is what causes that tight, dry, squeaky-clean feeling after washing, and over time it weakens the skin barrier.

Cetaphil's Gentle Skin Cleanser does not foam and does not rely on sulphates. It uses mild, skin-safe surfactants that lift away dirt, oil, and makeup without disturbing the skin's natural moisture barrier. The result is clean skin that still feels soft and hydrated after washing, not stripped or tight.

For people dealing with redness, dryness, sensitivity, or irritation caused by other face washes, Cetaphil is one of the first brands dermatologists recommend switching to. It is also widely recommended for people undergoing treatments like retinol, AHA, BHA, or prescription acne medications, because it is gentle enough to not add further irritation to already sensitized skin.

The Gentle Skin Cleanser is suitable for twice-daily use. It can be used as a standalone cleanser or as part of a double-cleansing routine after an oil-based cleanser. It is safe for use around the eyes and does not sting. For most skin types, it delivers consistent, reliable results without the need to switch products seasonally.

Is Cetaphil Good for Oily Skin?


Yes. A common misconception is that Cetaphil is only for dry or sensitive skin. The brand has a dedicated range specifically formulated for oily, combination, and acne-prone skin that addresses excess sebum without over-drying.

The Cetaphil Oily Skin Cleanser is a lightweight, water-based formula that removes excess oil, unclogs pores, and controls shine throughout the day. Unlike harsh foaming cleansers that strip the skin and trigger rebound oil production, the Oily Skin Cleanser balances sebum levels without disrupting the skin barrier. Used twice daily, it reduces the frequency of breakouts and keeps the T-zone matte without making the cheeks feel dry.

For more active oily and acne-prone skin, the Cetaphil PRO Oil Control Foam Face Wash is a stronger option. It contains zinc technology that helps regulate oil production at the source. The lightweight foam texture feels refreshing on oily skin and rinses off cleanly without leaving any residue. It is non-comedogenic, meaning it will not block pores, and it is dermatologist-tested for acne-prone skin.

Both products are free from harsh alcohol, sulphates, and artificial fragrance, making them suitable for people who need oil control without the irritation that usually comes with acne-targeted face washes.

If you have oily or combination skin and are unsure which Cetaphil cleanser to use, the Oily Skin Cleanser is the gentler everyday option, while the PRO Oil Control Foam Face Wash is better suited for skin that is actively breaking out or producing excessive shine.

How to Use Cetaphil Coupons & Promo Codes

How to Use Cetaphil Cleanser

Before applying any coupon, it helps to know which Cetaphil cleanser is right for your skin type so you buy the correct product and get the full benefit of your discount.

How to Use Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser: The Gentle Skin Cleanser is designed for normal to dry or sensitive skin. Apply a small amount to damp skin using your fingertips. Massage gently in circular motions for 20 to 30 seconds. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water or wipe off with a soft cotton pad. No lathering is needed. Use morning and night as part of your daily routine. Follow with a Cetaphil moisturizer on damp skin for best results.

How to Use Cetaphil Oily Skin Cleanser: Wet your face with lukewarm water. Apply a pea-sized amount of the Cetaphil Oily Skin Cleanser and lather gently. Massage across the face focusing on the T-zone and areas prone to excess oil. Rinse completely and pat dry with a clean towel. Do not scrub. Use twice daily. Follow with an oil-free moisturizer if your skin feels tight after cleansing.

How to Apply Cetaphil Cleanser Without Water: If you are travelling or do not have access to water, apply the Gentle Skin Cleanser directly on dry skin without wetting it first. Massage gently and wipe off with a soft cotton pad. This no-rinse method works specifically with the Gentle Skin Cleanser and is not recommended for the Oily Skin Cleanser or foam-based variants.
