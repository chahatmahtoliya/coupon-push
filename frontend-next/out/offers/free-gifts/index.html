Calculate the extra spend before chasing a gift

Use your planned qualifying basket, not a basket inflated to reach the promotion. Extra spend is the qualifying threshold minus your planned basket, with a minimum of zero. Check whether the store measures that threshold before or after discounts.

Illustrative example: moving a Rs.600 basket to a Rs.799 threshold requires Rs.199 more qualifying spend. A condition saying above Rs.799 can require more. The example does not establish any store’s current eligibility rule.

A gift’s retail value is not a refund

An advertised gift value is not money deducted from the checkout total. Check whether you want the item, whether it is added automatically or must be selected, and whether substitutions or stock limits apply.

Two gift tiers are not automatically cumulative. Check which tier the cart receives, and review whether cancelling or returning a qualifying item changes the gift eligibility.

Questions before you order

Do these free gifts require a purchase?

The promotions in this collection are purchase-linked. Each listing shows the advertised threshold or multi-buy condition. They are not unconditional giveaways.

Does no coupon code mean the gift is guaranteed?

No. The banner may not provide a code, but eligible products, qualifying spend and stock can still apply. Confirm the gift in the order summary before paying.

Before checkout

  • Read above versus at least in the qualifying spend condition.
  • Confirm whether gift tiers replace each other or combine.
  • Check the free item appears in the cart and does not add a charge.
  • Review stock, substitutions and the merchant’s return conditions.
