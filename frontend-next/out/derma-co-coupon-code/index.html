About derma co

The Derma Co is one of India's fastest-growing science-backed skincare brands, founded in 2020 under Honasa Consumer Limited. Built on the principle that effective skincare should be accessible, the brand formulates every product with clinically studied active ingredients - niacinamide, salicylic acid, vitamin C, kojic acid, hyaluronic acid, AHA-BHA blends, and retinol - each at concentrations that actually deliver visible results.

The brand targets real, common Indian skin concerns: acne and pimples, post-acne marks, pigmentation and dark spots, open pores, dull skin, hair fall, and dandruff. Every product in the lineup is dermatologist-tested and designed to be safe for daily use across all skin types.

Popular product ranges include the Sali-Cinamide Anti-Acne Face Wash, the 2% Kojic Acid Serum for pigmentation, the 20% Vitamin C Face Serum for brightening, and SPF 50 PA++++ sunscreens formulated specifically for Indian climate and skin. The brand also runs a free skin assessment on its website that generates a personalized skincare routine, which makes it a go-to destination for first-time active ingredient users.
