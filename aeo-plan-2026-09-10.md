# CouponPush AEO plan

Prepared 10 September 2026. Market: India, English, INR. Planning assumption: one developer and a part-time editor. This is a proposed execution plan; no production settings, content, or outreach were changed.

## Objective and approach

Make CouponPush a cited source for current, evidence-backed shopping savings in web-connected AI answers, then convert useful referrals into merchant visits and attributable orders. There is no universal LLM ranking position. Track citations, accurate mentions, referrals and commercial outcomes separately. Training-data inclusion is not the objective for short-lived coupons.

The strategic bet is exact eligibility and observed savings: answer which offer applies to a specific shopper and cart, with its source, conditions and check date. Treat this as a hypothesis to test, not an established ranking formula.

## What we already have

- `seo-pseo-growth-plan-2026-09-05.md` covers verification, merchant clusters and source-backed content, but does not define a dedicated AI citation baseline or experiment.
- The September 9 audit records 57 indexable pages in the local export and working SEO checks. These are export results, not proof of Google indexing or deployment. Do not carry forward September 5's counts as current findings.
- `frontend-next/public/robots.txt` already allows public pages and declares both sitemaps. Adding bot names alone is not an access fix. Verify the deployed file and CDN behavior.
- Build scripts already prepare the catalog and run SEO validation. Extend these rather than create another content pipeline.
- Store pages already generate CollectionPage/BreadcrumbList markup. Validate actual output rather than assume schema is absent.
- The current Coupon TypeScript interface has `is_verified` and `updated_at`, but no dedicated source, check method, checked-at or eligibility fields. Some evidence is embedded in free text; structured records are the next substantial improvement.
- Individual coupon pages are intentionally noindex in the latest audit. Put citation-worthy information on canonical merchant pages rather than exposing every thin coupon URL.

No authenticated GSC, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics, CDN logs or affiliate reports were inspected for this plan. Current AI visibility is unknown, not zero. Today's public robots fetch failed through the research tool; that does not establish a crawler block. The September 9 deployment/access findings require a fresh production check.

## Days 1–7: eligibility and baseline

Developer and site owner:

1. Verify the actual production release, canonical redirects, sitemap URLs, HTTP status, robots and snippet eligibility for the six pilot pages. Reuse `check-live-seo.py`, `audit-live-indexing.py` and `check-seo.py`. Check initial HTML contains the offer terms without clicks or a client-side API refresh. Preserve private/admin exclusions.
2. Inspect CDN logs for verified Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot requests. Confirm useful public HTML is returned without challenges. A spoofed User-Agent test cannot establish real crawler access; validate identities using providers' published mechanisms/IP ranges. Keep any exceptions scoped to verified crawlers and public routes.
3. Confirm Google Search Console Settings → Search generative AI includes the site, including inherited property settings. Inclusion is the documented default, but the actual property needs checking. [Google control documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024).
4. Keep search crawling and model training decisions separate. OAI-SearchBot controls ChatGPT search crawling; GPTBot governs potential training use. PerplexityBot serves search discovery. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Perplexity crawler documentation](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers).
5. Export available Google generative AI impressions by page/country/date and Bing AI citation data. Save normal organic traffic alongside them. If a report is unavailable or below its reporting threshold, record unavailable, not zero. [Google AI report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139), [Bing AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c).
6. Establish a repeatable prompt panel and analytics events before editing pilot content.

Acceptance: a recorded eligibility/access result for every pilot URL; dated baseline exports or an explicit unavailable status; no unexplained production noindex/canonical mismatch on pilot pages. Do not remove intentional quality exclusions just to increase page count.

## Days 8–30: six merchant pages worth citing

Start with the existing beauty cluster. These are editorial priorities from our earlier plan and current indexability inventory, not measured search-demand rankings. Validate current offer evidence before publishing improvements.

| Merchant | Existing canonical page | Main question to answer |
|---|---|---|
| Cetaphil | `/store/cetaphil-coupon-code/` | Which India retailer offers a supported saving on the same product and size? Clearly say when no public code is confirmed. |
| Dot & Key | `/store/dot-key/` | Which first-order or bundle offer fits this cart? Avoid reviving the empty duplicate profile. |
| The Derma Co | `/store/the-derma-co/` | What are the exclusions and eligibility for the currently documented offer? Audit the other Derma profile before consolidation. |
| Foxtale | `/store/foxtale/` | Does the offer apply to a new or returning customer, and at what minimum spend? |
| Minimalist | `/store/minimalist/` | Is buying directly or through a retailer cheaper for the same item, including shipping? |
| Plum | `/store/plum/` | What does the current bundle or coupon actually save on eligible items? |

Developer: extend database → admin/import validation → API → types → prepared snapshot → initial HTML. Store `source_url`, `source_type`, `checked_at`, `check_method`, `verification_status`, reviewer, country/currency, minimum spend, saving cap, audience, channel, payment requirement, exclusions, stacking rules and expiry. Unknown remains unknown. An affiliate destination is not automatically the source of the terms.

Editor: populate evidence for all featured pilot offers. Separate merchant-advertised, checkout-tested, user-reported, failed and expired statuses. A failed cart does not prove a code fails for every shopper. `updated_at` must not masquerade as a checkout verification timestamp.

Each merchant page should provide:

1. A short direct answer based on current evidence: best supported offer for a stated scenario, key conditions, and last check. No fixed word-count target.
2. A readable comparison table: code/deal, saving, minimum spend, cap, customer eligibility, app/web, exclusions and expiry.
3. Primary-source links and visible verification method. Keep the source next to the relevant claim.
4. A genuine example cart where testing is possible: identical SKU/size, subtotal, discount, shipping and final payable amount. Disclose location/channel and timestamp; never invent successful checkout evidence.
5. Merchant-specific redemption instructions and supported reasons a code may fail. Label unknown terms explicitly.
6. Reviewer identity, editorial policy link, affiliate disclosure and an implemented correction/report route.

Example answer structure, not a live offer claim: state the checked date, merchant, eligible customer/cart, applicable offer, capped saving, channel and evidence method in plain language. Distinguish immediate discounts from future cashback and rewards; do not add them together as immediate savings.

Keep terms and real code text in accessible HTML. Code-copy interactions can remain, but a thin noindex modal/detail route must not be the sole location of useful terms. Make the summary, schema, table and metadata use the same validated records. Use truthful existing schema; do not force coupon pages into Product markup or add invented ratings.

Acceptance: all featured pilot offers have a source and evidence status; unknown conditions are labeled; visible and machine-readable facts agree; initial HTML is useful; supported expiry changes are reflected in the published release. Target at least 95% of active pilot offers within the agreed review window. This is an internal maintenance target, not a ranking requirement.

## Days 31–60: comparison evidence and credibility

Editor:

- Publish one original savings study from at least 20 documented eligible-cart checks across the pilot merchants, if feasible. Explain sample selection, dates, exclusions and method. Report observations for the tested sample only; do not claim market-wide success rates.
- Add at most two useful guides supported by current evidence, such as first-order versus returning-customer eligibility and whether a bundle beats a percentage discount. Use existing merchant sections or blog pages when they already own the intent; do not create a page for every prompt variant.
- Link the beauty hub to merchants and the relevant guides; link guides back to the underlying offer evidence.
- Improve the existing About page with real ownership/editor identities, contact information, verification methodology, corrections and disclosure. Audit any existing self-ranking comparison article for unsupported claims.
- Prepare tailored pitches for relevant shopping publishers or merchant partners using the original findings. Send outreach only under separate authorization. Seek real editorial references; avoid bought mentions, fabricated reviews and forum seeding.

Developer: add rebuild/expiry monitoring to the current static pipeline. Trigger a release after approved material changes and scheduled expiry checks; alert on failures and verify deployed freshness. Update dates only when facts change or a real check occurs.

Review priority offers daily during sales and twice weekly otherwise; process explicit expiry at the stated deadline. When fresh checking is unavailable, remove the unsupported current claim or mark the evidence stale. Budget roughly 4–6 developer days for the first implementation plus 30–60 editorial minutes per working day for the pilot, then revise from measured workload. These are planning estimates.

## Days 61–90: evaluate, improve, then expand

Compare pilot pages with their baseline and similar unchanged pages where available. Keep an intervention log; promotions, seasonality and model changes can confound the results. A small before/after sample is directional evidence, not proof of causation.

- No verified crawler access: fix eligibility/delivery first.
- Crawled but not indexed: inspect quality, duplication, canonicals and indexing evidence.
- Indexed but rarely cited: inspect which sources answer the prompt better, improve missing evidence, and allow time for recrawling.
- Citations that misstate the offer: clarify the source page and time/eligibility boundaries, then recheck; record the error separately from citation success.
- Citations without visits: examine whether the cited answer satisfies the entire task and whether the page offers useful next steps. Do not hide terms to force clicks.
- Visits without merchant clicks: examine offer fit and usability. Merchant clicks without orders require affiliate/tracking checks before content conclusions.

Expand by at most four maintained merchants only after the six-page cohort meets freshness and data-quality targets for four consecutive weeks. Prefer topics evidenced by queries, observed citations and commercial results. Continue the current cohort if results are too sparse to support expansion.

## Weekly measurement protocol

Use 24 fixed non-CouponPush-branded prompts: four questions for each pilot merchant. Cover current offers, customer/cart eligibility, redemption failure and an applicable comparison. Example: “Which Foxtale offer applies to a returning customer with a ₹1,000 cart in India?” The question is a test scenario, not a claim that such an offer exists.

Run the panel in fresh conversations on ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and Copilot: 96 observations per weekly wave. Keep India/English settings, search mode and prompt wording consistent, and record date/time, available model/version, response, cited URLs and errors. Use a monthly repeat on a subset to assess variability. A missing answer or tool failure is not a completed non-citation. Do not tell the engine to cite CouponPush in discovery tests; run branded/source-fetch diagnostics separately. API results, if used, are a separate series from consumer-product results.

| Metric | Definition and limitation |
|---|---|
| Panel citation rate | Completed answers linking CouponPush / completed observations, reported per engine and prompt group. A sample metric, not global AI market share. |
| Accurate citation rate | Completed answers linking CouponPush and faithfully representing the relevant offer / completed observations. Also record factual errors among cited answers. |
| Mention rate | Answers mentioning CouponPush without requiring a link; keep separate from citations. |
| First-party visibility | Google AI impressions and Bing cited pages/citations from their respective reports. Do not add impressions and citations into one total. |
| Observed AI referrals | Sessions with identifiable AI referrers/source parameters. Missing referrers cause undercounting; do not attribute all direct traffic to AI. |
| Useful actions | Successful `coupon_copy`, `merchant_click` and real `coupon_report` events, deduplicated and without personal data. Existing backend click counts are not purchases. |
| Commercial results | Attributable approved affiliate orders and commission where permitted subIDs/reports exist. Unknown attribution remains unknown. |
| Freshness | Active pilot offers checked within the agreed window / all active pilot offers, with expired claims and failed rebuilds reported separately. |

Set citation and revenue growth targets after two baseline waves and first-party data review. Do not promise a percentage increase, fixed LLM position or 90-day revenue amount before a baseline exists.

## Work to deprioritize

Do not lead with `llms.txt`, special “AI schema,” bulk FAQs, mass city/month/prompt pages, or attempts to get short-lived codes memorized during model training. Google explicitly says it does not use llms.txt for ranking and emphasizes useful original material over these tactics. [Current Google AI optimization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

Google's older AI features page describes reporting differently from its newer help documentation. This plan follows the newer dedicated Search Console control/report documentation checked on September 10, while leaving actual property availability unverified.
