# CouponPush partner surfaces

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing CouponPush visual world without replacing it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Give brand representatives one clear, persistent place to manage reviewed store and offer submissions.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve CouponPush's Satoshi sans, navy text, blue actions, white/pale-blue surfaces and native controls.

STORY: Create an account, verify email, save details and offers, submit, then follow feedback and publication.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Public header above a left-led landing explanation and right process list. Workspace header above side navigation and a primary task area; mobile form and offer actions come first.

FORM: Explicitly scoped incumbent-product extension; no new-world seed or open-ended composition round applies. Flat offer/activity rows, readable states and inline editors.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Direction contract: Use the current Satoshi/site sans, navy text, restrained blue actions, white and pale blue surfaces, a compact scale for labels, and clear native form controls. The landing page explains the reviewed submission process. The account form is the first mobile task. The dashboard uses one primary work area with persistent navigation and flat offer/activity rows. Mobile exposes offer status and actions without requiring a hidden horizontal scroll. No decorative illustrations, fabricated social proof, traffic promises, or purchase metrics.

Quality bar: A first-time representative can locate account creation, understand email verification and brand authorization, save and resume a draft, add coupons or no-code deals, submit for review, find actionable feedback, and distinguish approval from completed publication. A returning representative can find active/expired/in-review offers and understand when editing is locked and how to return to review status. Forms have named controls, visible focus, field errors, and useful retry/empty states. Data shown in local captures is explicitly a test fixture in the review packet, not a production performance claim.

Authority: the user's approved feature flow and existing CouponPush components/tokens. Hosting PHP mail is confirmed. The public launch flag remains off until backend configuration and migration are ready. No separate comp or concept image was used.
